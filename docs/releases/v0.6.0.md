# v0.6.0 · 剧场、表情与音乐 / Theatre, expressions and music

## 中文

- **裙摆飘动。** 裙摆、发梢和尾巴轻轻摆动，脸部不会变形；桌宠和剧场都生效。可在 设置 › 陪伴 › 裙摆飘动 中关闭；不支持 WebGL 或系统开启减弱动效时自动使用静态立绘。
- **六个新表情。** 得意、害羞、抓狂、惊讶、满足、开心。剧场里会根据每一页台词自动换表情，小跳一下并在头顶冒出情绪符号（♥ ✦ ♪ 💧 ! ? 💢）。日常也会用到：摸头（满足或害羞）、回答完成、梁文峰 / 梁文谷播报、余额偏低以及偶尔的小表情。还有个小彩蛋：一直戳她试试。
- **剧场场景。** 四套插画背景（和室书房·夜、科技机房、午后咖啡厅、海边黄昏），也可以跟随时间自动切换（默认），或上传自己的图片、保留「透明」显示 DSH。切换时 0.7 秒交叉淡入，不闪白。
- **背景音乐。** 「日常轻快」「宁静闲聊」「深夜治愈」由插件实时演奏、无缝循环，也可以添加本地音乐。离开剧场或页面隐藏时自动淡出并释放音频。
- **GalGame 对话框。** 深蓝毛玻璃对话框与名字标签；文字逐字淡入，句末鲸尾点击提示；快进（按住 Ctrl 也可以）。左上角按钮打开音乐、背景和剧场设置，并移到 DSH 标题栏下方，不再遮挡菜单。
- **自动记住。** 背景、音乐、音量和裙摆飘动开关都会保存；你的图片和音乐只保存在本机浏览器。
- **更小的安装包。** 立绘改为 WebP 内嵌，运行包从 18.9 MB 降到约 7.6 MB。

构建、类型检查、运行包语法及 **107 项测试通过**；已在 Chromium（含无 WebGL 环境）中验证裙摆飘动、表情切换、戳戳彩蛋、交叉淡入、音乐淡出与挂起、刷新后恢复设置和本地文件。

下载 **dsh-whale-pet-v0.6.0.zip**，完整解压覆盖原插件目录，运行 install.bat，在 DSH 按 **Ctrl+R** 刷新。原有偏好保留。

## English

- **Swaying clothes.** Her skirt, hair ends and tail sway gently while her face stays exactly as drawn, on the desktop and in the theatre. Turn it off in Settings › Companionship › Swaying clothes; without WebGL or with reduced motion she uses the still image.
- **Six new expressions.** Smug, shy, frustrated, surprised, proud and happy. In the theatre each page of dialogue sets her expression, with a small hop and a mood mark above her head (♥ ✦ ♪ 💧 ! ? 💢). Daily life uses them too: head pats (proud or shy), finished answers, peak/off-peak announcements, low balance and the occasional idle glance. And a small easter egg: try poking her again and again.
- **Theatre scenes.** Four illustrated backgrounds (study at night, server room, afternoon café, seaside dusk), following the time of day by default, plus your own picture or Transparent to keep DSH visible. Switching crossfades in 0.7 s without flashing.
- **Background music.** Everyday Breeze, Quiet Chat and Late-night Comfort, performed live by the plugin with seamless loops, or your own audio file. Music fades out and releases the audio device when you leave the theatre or hide the page.
- **Visual-novel dialogue.** Deep-blue glass box with a name tag; characters fade in one by one with a whale-tail click-wait mark; Skip (or hold Ctrl). The chips for music, background and theatre settings now sit below the DSH title bar instead of covering its menu.
- **Remembered.** Background, music, volume and the swaying-clothes switch are saved; your picture and music stay in this browser.
- **Smaller package.** Artwork is embedded as WebP; the bundle drops from 18.9 MB to about 7.6 MB.

Build, type checking, bundle syntax and **107 tests passed**. Swaying clothes, expression changes, the poke easter egg, crossfades, music fade-out and suspend, and restoring settings and local files after reload were verified in Chromium.

Extract **dsh-whale-pet-v0.6.0.zip** over the existing plugin directory, run install.bat and press **Ctrl+R** in DSH. Preferences are retained.
