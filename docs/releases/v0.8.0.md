# v0.8.0 · 探头陪伴与更流畅的 Live2D / Peeking and a smoother Live2D

## 中文

- **从右边探头陪你。** 悬停菜单里的「探头」（显示快捷键）或 **Alt+P**（可在设置里重新录制）：她躲到窗口右边，只探出半个身子，不挡对话和输入框；再按一次回到原来的位置。Live2D 模型斜着探出、一只手拉住边框，手臂从肩到手连成一体；没有模型时，她先走到边上，再换成专门的探头插图。被晃晕时没法探头，会提示原因。
- **新插图**：被拎起来、从右边框探头，没有导入 Live2D 模型时使用。
- **头顶常显**计费时段和余额，读不到余额时显示“余额 —”。
- **Live2D 更生动**：聊天时会倾听、点头、边说边换重心；待机小动作和长表演更多，按时段变化；游荡时“游过去”；拎起时双腿并拢、小腿晃荡，落地屈膝缓冲。去掉了别扭的叉腰。
- **挂机后不再一直卡。** 修复后台低帧率把帧预算锁在 15 帧的问题。新增 **省电模式**（设置 › 陪伴）：最高 30 帧，待机 20 帧，后台 10 帧。

下载 `dsh-whale-pet-v0.8.0.zip`，完整解压到固定目录，运行 `install.bat`，在 DSH 按 Ctrl+R。从 v0.7.0 覆盖升级会保留设置和已导入的模型。

**模型不随包提供，也不是必需项。** 请从[神宫凉子原发布渠道](https://space.bilibili.com/13737731)下载，再在 设置 › 角色模型 导入。原画及拆分：神宫凉子；Live2D 建模：霓虹猫_NeonNeko。

验证：构建、严格类型检查、173 项 Node 回归测试；真实模型在模拟 DSH 页面中检查了探头、拎起、悬停菜单和插图模式。实机 DSH 中的长时间挂机流畅度和 GPU 帧耗时仍需用户验收。

## English

- **Peek from the right edge.** Peek in the hover menu (its shortcut is shown) or **Alt+P**, which can be re-recorded in Settings: she hides behind the right edge, half out of sight and clear of the conversation and the composer; press again to bring her back. The Live2D model leans out holding the edge with the arm in one piece from shoulder to hand; without the model she walks to the edge and turns into a dedicated peeking illustration. While she is dizzy she cannot go, and says so.
- **New illustrations** for being picked up and for peeking, used without a Live2D model.
- **Chips always visible:** pricing period and balance; an unreadable balance shows “Balance —”.
- **Livelier Live2D:** she listens, nods and shifts her weight while you chat; more idle moments and longer pieces that follow the time of day; she swims across when wandering; legs close and dangle when picked up, with a cushioned landing. The odd hand-on-hip pose is gone.
- **Smooth after a long idle.** Fixes background frames locking the frame budget at 15 fps. New **Power saving** switch (Settings › Companionship): at most 30 fps, 20 while idle, 10 in the background.

Extract the complete `dsh-whale-pet-v0.8.0.zip`, run `install.bat` and refresh DSH. Upgrading over v0.7.0 keeps your settings and an imported model. The Live2D model is optional and not redistributed; obtain it from its author and import it in Settings.

Validation: build, strict type checks and 173 Node tests; the real model was checked on a simulated DSH page for peeking, carrying, the hover menu and illustration mode. Long-idle smoothness and GPU frame times in a real DSH window still need acceptance by the owner.
