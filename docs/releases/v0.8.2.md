# v0.8.2 · 桌面模式预览 / Desktop mode preview

## 中文

- **桌面模式（预览）：** 大肥鱼可以离开 DSH 窗口，待在系统桌面上：透明、始终置顶、鼠标不在她身上时点击会穿透到下面。DSH 开着时两边自动连接，你在 DSH 里提问，桌面上的她也会跟着思考、回答、干饭，并显示余额。
- **在桌面上直接提问：** 按 **Alt+Shift+T**、点托盘菜单或她的悬停菜单里的“剧场”，就能在桌面上打开剧场。问题交给 DSH，用它自己的输入框发出，回答逐字显示在桌面剧场里，不用切到 DSH 窗口。DSH 没连上或没有打开的对话时，草稿会保留并说明原因。
- **探头更清爽：** 探头时头顶只留“设置｜回来 Alt+P”一个小块，下面是峰谷时间和余额；探头时打开设置，她留在原地，面板在她左边。回来的路上她先转正身体，再正着走完剩下的路。
- **探头的手臂（Live2D）：** 小臂和手画在大臂上面，小臂头正好接在大臂末端；回来时手不会先出现在裙子前面再瞬移到后面。
- **新的待机表情（Live2D）：** 偶尔会露出半闭着眼、有点得意的表情。
- **剧场提示看得清：** 提示改成白字，显示在对话框上方，不再压在按钮上。
- **新的操作说明：** [操作说明](../USER_GUIDE.zh-CN.md)；README 只保留最新版本介绍，中文在前。

**插件：** 下载 **dsh-whale-pet-v0.8.2.zip**，解压到原来的插件目录覆盖，双击 **install.bat**（目录没变可以跳过），在 DSH 按 Ctrl+R。设置、互动记录和导入的模型都会保留。

**桌面版：** 下载 **dsh-whale-pet-desktop-v0.8.2-win-x64.zip**，解压到任意文件夹，双击 **DaFeiYu.exe**，不需要 Node.js。Windows 提示“未知发布者”时点“更多信息” › “仍要运行”。要和 DSH 联动，DSH 里的插件也需要是 v0.8.2。桌面版有自己的设置，Live2D 模型需要在它的设置里再导入一次；模型不随下载包提供。退出：右键托盘里的蓝色小圆图标。

桌面模式还是预览：桌面剧场的“回顾”暂时看不到 DSH 里的历史消息；DSH 和桌面版目前可以同时显示她。

验证：213 项 Node 测试、构建、两套类型检查，Windows 上的打包与桌面版构建检查；桌面剧场的输入、发送、回顾、剧场设置、Esc 和退出用真实鼠标键盘事件在 Electron 中检查过，提问经过模拟的 DSH 页面送达。

## English

- **Desktop mode (preview):** 大肥鱼 can leave the DSH window and sit on your desktop: transparent, always on top, and clicks go through unless the pointer is on her. With DSH open the two connect automatically; ask in DSH and she thinks, answers and takes her rice break on the desktop too, with your balance.
- **Ask from the desktop:** **Alt+Shift+T**, the tray menu or Theatre in her hover menu opens the theatre on the desktop. Questions go to DSH, which sends them through its own composer, and the answer streams into the desktop theatre without switching windows. When DSH is not connected or has no conversation open, the draft is kept and she says why.
- **Tidier peeking:** while she peeks, one small “Settings | Come back Alt+P” block sits above her head with the pricing period and balance below it; Settings opened while peeking leaves her at the edge with the panel to her left. Coming back, she straightens up first and walks the rest of the way upright.
- **The peeking arm (Live2D):** the forearm and hand are drawn over the upper arm, its top end on the upper arm's lower end; coming back, the hand no longer jumps from in front of the skirt to behind it.
- **A new idle face (Live2D):** now and then a heavy-lidded, slightly pleased look.
- **Readable theatre notices:** white text, above the dialogue box instead of over its buttons.
- **New user guide:** [user guide](../USER_GUIDE.md); the README shows only the latest release, Chinese first.

**Plugin:** download **dsh-whale-pet-v0.8.2.zip**, extract it over your plugin folder, run **install.bat** (optional if the folder did not move) and press Ctrl+R in DSH. Preferences, discoveries and the imported model are kept.

**Desktop app:** download **dsh-whale-pet-desktop-v0.8.2-win-x64.zip**, extract it anywhere and double-click **DaFeiYu.exe**; no Node.js needed. If Windows says “Unknown publisher”, choose More info › Run anyway. To connect with DSH, the plugin inside DSH must be v0.8.2 too. The desktop app has its own settings; import the Live2D model once more there (the model is not included). Quit from the small blue round tray icon.

Desktop mode is a preview: Log in the desktop theatre does not show DSH's earlier messages yet, and DSH and the desktop app can both show her at the same time.

Validation: 213 Node tests, build, both TypeScript checks, Windows packaging and desktop build checks; the desktop theatre's typing, sending, Log, theatre settings, Esc and Exit were checked in Electron with real mouse and keyboard events, with questions delivered through a simulated DSH page.
